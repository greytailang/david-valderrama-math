Research

2026 · Archive for Mathematical Logic · Online First

Splitting families, reaping families and families of permutations associated with asymptotic density

Identifies the zero-density splitting and reaping numbers with the covering and uniformity numbers of the meager ideal, respectively. The paper also studies cardinal invariants defined by families of permutations that change densities.

Read the published version for free ↗Journal article ↗arXiv:2506.21059 ↗

2025 · The Journal of Symbolic Logic · First View

Cardinal Invariants Related to Density

Uses forcing to separate density-based variants of the splitting, reaping, and independence numbers from classical cardinal invariants. The paper also connects these variants to the σ-ideal generated by closed null sets.
